Contact Gry

You are welcome to call or send an SMS.

There is no need to find the perfect words. A brief message saying that you are interested in arranging a session, or would like to ask a practical question, is enough.

Individual psychotherapy

You are welcome to get in touch if you are considering individual therapy.

You may already know what you want to talk about. You may also simply sense that something has become difficult, and that it may help to speak with someone.

Both are good reasons to begin.

Collaboration under strain

You are also welcome to get in touch about an important professional collaboration that is under pressure.

A brief message is enough. You do not need to explain the whole situation before contact. The first conversation is there to clarify what is happening, whether this setting may be useful, and whether further conversations feel right.

Practical questions

You can also call or send an SMS if you have a practical question about prices, session length or arranging a time.
