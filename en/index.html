Psychotherapy in Copenhagen

As a trained psychotherapist, I offer therapeutic conversations in Copenhagen: a calm and supportive space to explore what has become difficult, and what may need attention now.

You may be facing something immediate, carrying something that has built up over time, or noticing patterns that are hard to change on your own.

You do not need to know exactly where to begin. We can start with what feels most present now, and take time to understand it together.

Therapeutic conversations

Psychotherapy can be a way to pause and look more carefully at what is happening in your life.

Some people come with a clear concern. Others simply sense that something has become harder to carry. Both are welcome.

In our conversations, there is room to speak openly, without pressure to perform or find the perfect words.

When an important collaboration is under strain

I also offer conversations for people in an important professional collaboration where communication, trust or shared responsibility has become difficult.

This may be relevant when the collaboration still matters, but the usual ways of speaking together are no longer working well enough.

The work is not about deciding who is right. It is about slowing the conversation down enough to understand what has become difficult, what each person needs to take responsibility for, and whether a more workable way forward is possible.

About Gry

My professional background includes work in social psychiatry, consultancy and therapeutic support for people affected by alcohol, substances and dependency.

Across these settings, I have worked with people in many different life situations. My approach is calm, attentive and grounded in respect for the pace of the person or collaboration in front of me.
