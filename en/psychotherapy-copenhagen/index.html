Individual psychotherapy in Copenhagen

Psychotherapy can offer a calm and supportive space to explore what has become difficult, and what may need attention now.

You may be dealing with stress, anxiety, loneliness, loss, relationship difficulties, dependency, or patterns that feel hard to change alone. You may also come without a clear explanation, but with a sense that something in your life has become more difficult to carry.

You do not need to have everything figured out before getting in touch. We can begin with what feels most present, and take time to understand it together.

What you can bring

People come to therapy for many different reasons.

You may be facing a life change, a crisis, a period of stress, or a feeling of being stuck. You may find yourself reacting in ways you do not fully understand, or repeating patterns that leave you tired, alone or uncertain about how to move forward.

Some concerns are easy to name. Others are harder to put into words. Both can be brought into the conversation.

How I work

My approach is grounded in calm attentiveness, patience and respect for your pace.

In our sessions, there is room to speak openly without pressure to perform, explain everything perfectly, or find the right words straight away.

The work is not about forcing quick answers. It is about listening carefully, understanding what is at stake, and gradually finding more sustainable ways of being with your experiences, your relationships and yourself.

When something is hard to carry alone

Therapy can be helpful when something has become difficult to manage on your own.

That might be a specific situation, such as a loss, a conflict, a change in work or family life, or a period of anxiety or stress. It might also be something quieter: a sense of unease, loneliness, dependency, shame, or a feeling that familiar patterns keep returning.

You are welcome to bring what is here now, even if it is not yet clear.

Beginning

There is no need to prepare the right story before you come.

A first conversation can simply be a place to begin. We can look at what brings you here, what feels most important now, and whether further conversations feel right.
