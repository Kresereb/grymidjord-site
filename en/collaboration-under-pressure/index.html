When an important collaboration is under pressure

An important collaboration can, over time, come under strain. It may be marked by misunderstandings, recurring tension, disappointment, or a sense that something essential between you has become harder to hold on to.

When the collaboration begins to take up too much space, cost too much energy, or affect working life more than is good, it can help to pause and look more calmly at what has happened and what is now needed.

These conversations offer a space for clarity, understanding, and a more workable way forward.

When the collaboration begins to cost more than it carries

An important collaboration can be central. It may be a relationship that carries responsibility, trust, professional judgement, and many everyday decisions. Precisely for that reason, it can also be difficult when something begins to slip.

Perhaps the conversations between you have become cautious or tense. Perhaps the same conflicts keep repeating themselves. Perhaps doubt, irritation, or a sense of no longer understanding one another as you once did has begun to grow.

Some feel it as unease before meetings, growing tiredness, or an increasing reluctance to be in contact. Others experience that the collaboration appears to function on the surface, while inwardly it has become fragile or stuck.

It can be difficult to find a place to talk about this, especially if you want to preserve both dignity, care, and professionalism. These conversations are not intended as quick fixes, but as a calm setting in which it becomes possible to understand what has come under strain and what may need to change.

The conversations may be relevant when

The conversations may be relevant when an important collaboration has been affected by, for example:

  • recurring conflict or stuck patterns
  • weakened trust
  • unclear roles, responsibilities, or expectations
  • disappointments or events that have never really been worked through
  • a sense of missing one another again and again
  • tensions that affect work, energy, or wellbeing
  • uncertainty about how the collaboration might continue in a more sustainable way

They may also be relevant if you cannot yet fully name the problem, but can sense that the relationship has become heavier, more fragile, or harder to be in.

How I work

I work calmly, attentively, and without rushing to conclusions. The aim is not to place blame, but to create a clearer view of what has happened between you, what keeps repeating itself, and what is at stake in the collaboration.

These conversations are intended as a shared space in which both people take part. When an important collaboration is under strain, the difficulties often show up in the interaction itself, and that is also where they are best explored.

There is room in the conversations both for what is concrete and for what may be harder to put into words. It may concern communication, trust, boundaries, patterns, disappointments, or strains that over time have come to shape the collaboration.

The focus here is not on romantic partnership, but on an important professional collaboration that has come under strain.

My focus is to create a calm and decent space where it becomes possible to understand what has become stuck and to find a more sustainable way of being in the collaboration.
