About Gry

I am a trained psychotherapist based in Copenhagen.

My work is grounded in calm attentiveness, patience and respect for the person or collaboration in front of me.

I see therapy as a place where there is time to pause, listen carefully and understand what may be difficult to see clearly when you are in the middle of it.

My background

My professional background includes work in social psychiatry, consultancy and therapeutic support for people affected by alcohol, substances and dependency.

These areas have given me experience with people in many different life situations, including periods marked by stress, uncertainty, loss, relationship difficulties, dependency, shame, overwhelm and recurring patterns that can feel hard to change alone.

They have also shaped the way I work: calmly, carefully and with attention to what is actually present, not only what is easiest to explain.

How I meet people

It is important to me that therapy does not become a performance.

You do not need to arrive with a complete explanation of what is wrong. You do not need to know the right words. Often, part of the work is simply finding a clearer language for something that has felt confusing, heavy or difficult to carry alone.

In our conversations, I listen for both what is said and what may be harder to say. We work at a pace that allows understanding to develop without unnecessary pressure.

Individual and relational work

I work with individuals who want support in understanding what has become difficult in their lives.

I also work with important professional collaborations where communication, trust or shared responsibility has come under strain.

In both forms of work, my focus is on creating a calm, serious and respectful setting where people can speak more clearly, listen more carefully and begin to understand what may be needed now.
