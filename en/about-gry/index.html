About Gry

I am a trained psychotherapist and offer conversations in Copenhagen.

For me, psychotherapy is a place where it becomes possible to pause and look more calmly and precisely at what has become difficult. It may be something that is pressing here and now, something that has been building over time, or patterns and experiences that have become hard to be with on your own.

My work is marked by attentiveness, calm, and respect for the fact that what matters cannot always be understood quickly. I place importance on creating a decent setting with room both for what is concrete and for what only gradually becomes possible to put into words.

My background

Professionally, I have experience from social psychiatry, as a consultant, and as an addiction counsellor. I also have experience with therapeutic conversations in which alcohol, substances, dependency, or the consequences of these are part of what is filling a person’s life.

Over the years, my work has given me experience with people in many different life situations. What runs through it all is an interest in how we live with strain, relationships, loss, unease, recurring patterns, and what can be difficult to understand or change on our own.

How I work

I work calmly, attentively, and without rushing to conclusions.

It matters to me that the conversations do not become a place where something has to be performed or explained in the right way. The aim is not to force anything forward, but to create a space where it becomes possible to understand more of what is at stake and to find a more sustainable way of being with it.

Some people come with something very concrete. Others come with a quieter sense that something in life has become harder to carry, harder to find a way through, or harder to stay in contact with. Both belong here.

What you can bring

You do not need a finished explanation before getting in touch.

It may be about stress or strain, life crises, anxiety or unease, loneliness, difficulties in relationships, dependency, or the experience of being stuck in patterns that have become hard to change on your own.

It may also be that you do not yet know exactly what the problem is, but can sense that something is asking for attention.
