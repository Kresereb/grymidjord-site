Prices & practical information

Here you can find information about prices, conversation formats, and practical details.

Individual sessions

An individual session lasts 60 minutes and costs DKK 950.

An extended individual session lasts 90 minutes and costs DKK 1,250.

Conversations when an important collaboration is under strain

An initial clarifying conversation costs DKK 1,950.

The first conversation is used to gain a calm and precise view of the situation and to assess whether it makes sense to continue in this format.

If a further course of conversations is needed, it is agreed on the basis of the situation, the format, and what is needed. For larger or more structured courses, an overall framework can be agreed.

Couples therapy

Couples therapy may also be an option and can be arranged when relevant.

Practical information

The conversations take place in Copenhagen.

If you are unsure which format fits best, you are welcome to call or send a text message.

Please give notice as early as possible if you need to cancel.
