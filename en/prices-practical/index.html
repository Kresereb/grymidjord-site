Prices & practical information

Sessions are held in Copenhagen.

You are welcome to call or send an SMS if you would like to arrange a session or ask a practical question.

Individual psychotherapy

60 minutes: 950 kr

90 minutes: 1,250 kr

A 60-minute session is suitable for most regular therapeutic conversations.

A 90-minute session may be useful when there is more to unfold, or when you know that a little more time would make the conversation feel less hurried.

Collaboration under strain

Clarifying first conversation: 1,950 kr

The first conversation is a paid, clarifying conversation for people in an important professional collaboration where communication, trust or shared responsibility has become difficult.

The purpose is to understand the situation, clarify what kind of support may be useful, and consider whether further conversations make sense.

There is no commitment to continue after the first conversation.

If further conversations seem useful, they currently continue at the same price per session: 1,950 kr.

If a different practical frame later becomes necessary, this will be agreed clearly in advance. This includes the focus of the work, session length and price.

A good fit matters

For therapy and collaboration work to be useful, there needs to be enough contact, respect and trust between Gry and the person or people taking part.

A first conversation also gives space to sense whether this feels possible.

If it does not feel like the right fit, it is better to know early. That can also be a useful clarification.

This applies both to individual therapy and to conversations about an important collaboration under strain.

Arranging a session

There is no need to write a long message.

You can simply send an SMS saying that you are interested in arranging a session, or that you would like to ask a practical question.

If you prefer, you are also welcome to call.

Before the first conversation

You do not need to prepare anything formal before the first session.

It is enough to arrive with what feels most important now, even if it is not yet clear or easy to explain.
